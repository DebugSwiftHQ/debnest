# Lead Engine changelog

What changed in the Lead Engine, newest first. Written from the history of its private source.

## October 2026: version 2 complete

**4 Oct**
- **Scripted mode:** automatic replies with no AI at all, for businesses that want instant answers but not an assistant.
- **Help for the team:** a "brief me" summary of any chat, suggested replies, and translation.
- Block a contact, and search inside every message.
- Send buttons, lists, location requests and contact cards.
- Phone numbers entered as country code plus number, and WhatsApp's own errors explained in plain words.
- The Back button walks back one step at a time on every screen.

**3 Oct**
- **WhatsApp templates:** write them, submit them for approval, and message a customer after the 24-hour window. Start a new chat by number.
- **Saved replies** (type `/` in a chat) and **snooze** a chat.
- **Assign a chat** to someone on the team, with a "Mine" tab.
- Roles: Owner, Manager, Agent and Viewer.
- Export leads to a spreadsheet.
- Edit the business's WhatsApp profile from the app.
- Business details questionnaire that drafts the assistant's setup.
- Bengali and Hindi typed in Latin letters get a reply in the customer's language.
- Team profile photos; crop and turn a photo before using it.

**1 to 2 Oct**
- **Playbooks:** each business sets its own reasons to write, questions, facts and rules, with templates for clinics, shops and salons, and a "try a message" box to test them.
- **Files, voice notes, reactions and forwarding** in the team inbox. Each photo or voice note is transcribed once.
- A health page showing that everything is connected.
- Nobody left unanswered: faster replies and more robust handling of busy moments.

## September 2026: version 2 begins

- **The team inbox app,** installable on phone and computer.
- **Instant notifications** for every message, with per-person choices.
- **Team accounts:** invite-only, with a team page and admin area.
- Customers who use WhatsApp usernames are answered instead of silently dropped.
- Wallpapers and contact info in chats.
- Customers can switch language just by asking.
- The assistant talks naturally in each language.
- No em dashes and no callback promises the business didn't make: enforced in code, not left to the prompt.

## August 2026

- **23 Aug: first live conversations.**
- Fixes from those first real conversations: no reciting the introduction, and a question is dropped rather than asked four times.
- Romanised Bengali answered in Bengali.
- Asking for a person works every time, typed or tapped.
- Spam protection, limits on endless chats, and guards against attempts to hijack the assistant.
- The assistant reads the business's website instead of keeping a stale copy of it.
- Full transcripts, and owner alerts that survive WhatsApp's 24-hour window.

## July 2026: first build

- The honesty guard: the assistant never quotes a price, never invents proof, and always says it is automated.
- The qualifying flow, the lead log and the one follow-up nudge.
- A way for the owner to take over a chat, and to hand it back.
- A qualified lead is never lost because a reply failed to send.
- Automated checks that run without touching a real WhatsApp account.
